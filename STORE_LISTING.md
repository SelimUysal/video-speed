## English (primary)

**Suggested store title** (also used in localized manifest): Video Speed – Playback Controller

**Short description** (localized manifest): Control HTML5 video speed with shortcuts, mouse wheel, and a draggable panel. Remember playback speed separately for each site.

**Detailed description:**

Take control of your video playback speed with Video Speed — a lightweight Chrome extension for HTML5 video players.

**Speed control without distractions**
- Adjust playback from 0.1× to 16× where the player supports it.
- Hover over the small on-video control panel for quick actions.
- Scroll on the speed number to increase or decrease speed without scrolling the page.
- Drag the panel to a convenient position; it fades when you're not using it.

**Remember each website independently**
Set YouTube to 3× and another video website to 16×. Each website keeps its own speed and panel position, including across browser restarts. Tabs of the same website share that site's preferences.

**Convenient keyboard shortcuts**
- Q: 3×, E: 2.5×
- D / S: increase / decrease speed; hold for continuous adjustment
- R: reset to 1×
- Z / X: rewind / forward 10 seconds

**Privacy-first approach**
Video Speed stores preferences locally in Chrome. It does not collect analytics, send your browsing activity to its developer, or require an account.

Works with many standard HTML5 video players. Some restricted players, protected content and website-specific controls may not support every feature.

Video Speed ile web üzerindeki HTML5 videolarını kolayca hızlandır veya yavaşlat. Küçük, sürüklenebilir kontrol paneli izlerken dikkatini dağıtmaz.

- Q: 3×, E: 2.5×
- D / S: artır / azalt; basılı tutarak sürekli değiştir
